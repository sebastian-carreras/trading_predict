# Artificial Intelligence for Decision-Making in the Purchase and Sale of Financial Assets
### Objective
Guide to decision-making in the purchase and sale of stocks, bonds, and currencies:
Improve accuracy when buying and selling securities on the stock market.
Minimize losses in movements by predicting and monitoring trends in real time.
Investment efficiency and resource optimization:
Apply predictive models that help manage capital efficiently, minimizing losses and maximizing profits.

### General introduction to the topic
In financial markets, decision-making regarding the purchase and sale of assets such as stocks, bonds, and currencies is complex and risky. Factors such as volatility, changes in economic policies, and price fluctuations create a challenging environment, where investors seek to minimize losses and maximize profits.

To this end, tools based on technical and fundamental analysis, as well as artificial intelligence (AI) models, will be explored. On the one hand, different models will be evaluated until those that best fit the information in the dataset are found. On the other hand, to discover technical and fundamental analysis techniques that help improve model predictions. The objective is to understand how these techniques and models can improve market decision-making, optimizing investment strategies through the integration of AI into capital and risk management.

These tools, combined with real-time market data, have the potential to significantly improve decision-making, providing entry and exit alerts, estimation of key values ​​such as "stop loss"1 and "take profit"2, and arbitrage opportunities.

### Detailed Description
This project proposes the development of an application that, using LSTM neural networks and other advanced deep learning techniques, can accurately predict the movements of financial assets in the market, including stocks, bonds, and currencies. The system will leverage both technical analysis (based on market indicators) and fundamental analysis (based on economic fundamentals) to improve the prediction of trends and movements. The application will allow users to configure automatic alerts to warn of potential entry or exit points in investments, with estimates for "stop loss" (to reduce losses) and "take profit" (to consolidate profits). Additionally, a module is planned to identify arbitrage opportunities between assets with similar characteristics, where users can take advantage of price differences between two correlated securities. The goal is to improve the accuracy and efficiency of investment decisions, optimizing the use of resources and maximizing financial returns.

### Data Availability
The effectiveness of the predictive models in this project depends largely on the quality and availability of data. Fortunately, financial markets are among the sectors with the greatest amount of available data. Historical and real-time data sources can be obtained from platforms such as Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, and specialized APIs such as Alpha Vantage, Quandl, or IEX Cloud. These sources offer data on historical prices, transaction volumes, technical indicators (such as moving averages and RSI), as well as fundamental economic information (such as earnings reports, balance sheets, and macroeconomic data). Many of these services offer free or low-cost access for research projects, making it easy to access reliable and up-to-date data.
